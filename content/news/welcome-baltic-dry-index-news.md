We're launching a **news section** to sit alongside our live [Baltic Dry Index](/) data, [historical charts](/bdi-chart-historical-data/) and [shipping tools](/tools/).

Here you'll find commentary on dry bulk freight markets — Capesize, Panamax, Supramax and Handysize — plus notes on iron ore, coal and grain flows that move the index.

For live figures and our data API, see the [homepage](/) and the [API page](/api/).
