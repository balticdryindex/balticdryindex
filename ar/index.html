الملخص الأسبوعي · 22 أبريل 2026
BDI يقفز 16.6% في أسبوع واحد مع بلوغ طلب كيبسايز أعلى مستوياته في أربعة أشهر
The Baltic Dry Index extended its rally for an eleventh consecutive session on Friday, climbing 1.7% to 2,567 — its highest reading since December 8. The capesize index jumped 2.5% to 4,128, driven by surging Brazilian iron ore export volumes and recovery in Chinese port activity.
